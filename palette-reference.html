Notes for Figma

Near-duplicates, fragmented tokens, and one-offs

Near-duplicates — don't flatten

  • Three violet text values: --brand-1 #7C3AED for icon fills and interactive tints, --brand-1-ink #6D28D9 for violet text on white surfaces, and --pill-ink #5B21B6 for text inside the lavender pill chips. They're intentionally distinct — mapping them to one value in Figma will break contrast in pill labels.
  • Lavender Tint #F1ECFE (= --pill) is hardcoded in 6+ component rules without referencing the token — values match, so you can safely unify to one Figma style. Worth flagging as a codebase cleanup.
  • Team B has two colors: #06B6D4 (cyan = identity color, the --accent-cyan token) and #3B82F6 (blue = scoreboard gradient endpoint only). In Figma, Team B's identity color is cyan. Blue is gradient-only.
  • Amber appears in three intensities: #F59E0B (--accent-amber, selected tile/Double Take border), #B45309 (Double Take label), #92400E (Double Take values). All three are meaningfully different — keep separate.

One-off / incidental — don't add to Figma

These appear in the CSS but are legacy, single-use, or accessibility-only. Not part of the brand system.
